# Store Performance Ledger Dashboard (Excel)

## Project Overview
An interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze over 100,000 retail transactions spanning 2019–2023 across 4 sales regions. The dashboard tracks core business KPIs, sales trends, regional distributions, and statistical profit bridges.

---

## Key Features & Visualizations
* **KPI Header & Summary Cards:** Live tracking of Total Sales (₹2.51B), Total Profit (₹375.5M), Profit Margin (15%), Avg Discount (25%), YoY Growth (1%), and Avg Order Value (₹25,084).
* **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic multi-tab filtering by Year and Region.
* **Trend & Category Analysis:** Sales Trend over time, Category distribution, and Top 10 Sub-Categories.
* **Advanced Profitability Visuals:** 
  * **Waterfall Chart:** YoY profit bridge (2019 → 2023).
  * **Box Plot:** Profit variance and outlier distribution across fulfillment modes.
  * **Scatter Plot:** Discount sensitivity vs. profit margin correlation.

## Tools & Concepts Used
* **Excel Features:** PivotTables, PivotCharts, Dynamic Slicers, Data Modeling, Custom Formats.
* **Charts:** Line, Stacked Bar, Donut, Waterfall, Box & Whisker, Scatter Plot.
* **Metrics Analyzed:** Sales Volume, Profit Margins, YoY Growth, Order Values, Regional Breakdown.
